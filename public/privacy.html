Your training data

Privacy notice · September 18, 2026

When you use the app without an account

The signed-out app is an unsaved preview. Your plan, workouts, check-ins, and preferences are held only in the open tab and disappear when it closes or reloads. Signing in opens your account; it does not automatically transfer preview changes.

When you sign in with Google

Google provides basic identity information, including your email and profile, to Supabase for your PANDR-5 account. PANDR-5 does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or calendars. Your Google password is handled by Google.

Your signed-in PANDR-5 profile and training records sync to the project’s Supabase database. Access rules restrict each account to its own records. The project administrator can manage the database; cloud storage is not end-to-end encrypted. Signing out returns to an unsaved preview. New training records are saved to your account, not to a local training database. The app retains the authentication session on your device so you can stay signed in.

Service providers

Vercel serves the web app, GitHub distributes the source and desktop downloads, Google handles Google sign-in, and Supabase provides accounts and cloud storage. These services process requests and may retain their own security and operational logs. PANDR-5 includes no advertising or analytics scripts and does not sell training data.

Keep a copy

Settings lets you export your complete profile as a JSON backup or detailed all-time CSV. Saving requires an internet connection. If a save fails, keep the tab open and retry or download a backup; unsaved changes are not stored locally. Free cloud services have storage limits and may pause inactive projects. Keep regular backups of information you want to retain.

Access and removal

You can explore an unsaved preview without signing in. To request removal of a cloud account, contact the project owner through the PANDR-5 repository. Do not post personal training records, passwords, or other private information in a public issue.

Protect shared devices with a screen lock. Older versions saved training on the device. Existing copies are left untouched and can be downloaded from Settings for recovery; new training is not written to them. People with access to your device profile may be able to read these older copies.
