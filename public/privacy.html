Your training data

Privacy notice · September 16, 2026

When you use the app without an account

Your plan, exercise library, workout history, notes, recovery check-ins, and preferences are saved on your device. PANDR-5 does not upload the local profile automatically when you sign in. Browser and desktop installations have separate local stores.

When you sign in with Google

Google provides basic identity information, including your email and profile, to Supabase for your PANDR-5 account. PANDR-5 does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or calendars. Your Google password is handled by Google.

Your signed-in PANDR-5 profile and training records sync to the project’s Supabase database. Access rules restrict each account to its own records. The project administrator can manage the database; cloud storage is not end-to-end encrypted. Signing out returns you to your separate local profile.

Service providers

Vercel serves the web app, GitHub distributes the source and desktop downloads, Google handles Google sign-in, and Supabase provides accounts and cloud storage. These services process requests and may retain their own security and operational logs. PANDR-5 includes no advertising or analytics scripts and does not sell training data.

Keep a copy

Settings lets you export your complete profile as a JSON backup or detailed all-time CSV. Browser data may be cleared or evicted. Free cloud services have storage limits and may pause inactive projects. Keep regular backups of information you want to retain.

Access and removal

You can use the app locally without signing in. To request removal of a cloud account, contact the project owner through the PANDR-5 repository. Do not post personal training records, passwords, or other private information in a public issue.

Protect shared devices with a screen lock. The app does not encrypt its local database, so people with access to your device profile may be able to read it.
