## Evidence hierarchy and inference

Priority was longitudinal training/MRI/ultrasound outcomes and syntheses where available; anatomy and joint actions supply the defensible common basis for many device variants. Some canonical rows inherit family-level evidence with an explicit extrapolation statement. The evidence record does **not** claim that every named variant has a trial. Most multi-muscle credits remain medium/low confidence because published results cannot distinguish, for example, exactly 0.25 from 0.5 sets of triceps work for a given press.

Neck, trunk isometrics, ballistic movements, rear-delt/upper-back partition, brachioradialis-versus-brachialis partition and uncommon machine paths have the greatest uncertainty. Some study samples are young/untrained and do not establish identical responses in trained, older or clinical populations. Anatomical group labels hide regional/head differences. Unknown machine design should lower confidence further in individual use.

No new precise EMG-to-FSA conversions were used. Passive tension, incidental bracing and antagonist activity do not automatically earn credit. No inference about medical rehabilitation or suitability for a particular injury follows from catalog inclusion.

## Coverage decisions

Retained 346 additions after family-level conceptual review; no equipment/grip combinatorial enumeration. Preserve broad legacy entries as calibration and add aliases rather than generating duplicate conventional dumbbell/barbell RDL rows. Separate material unilateral, supported, fixed-path, lever/cable, grip, depth and stance variants. Counterweight/weighted bodyweight modes share canonical IDs when mechanics are unchanged; variable band assistance can warrant a separate profile.

Uncovered anatomical targets (for example tibialis anterior, rotator cuff, serratus anterior, hip flexors and intrinsic hand/finger muscles) are documented rather than mislabeled. Their addition would require further explicit vocabulary approval. The approved abs/oblique/neck/wrist groups and all current 19 groups have at least one full-credit direct option.
