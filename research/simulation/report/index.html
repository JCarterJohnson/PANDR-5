What deserves attention

  • Ordinary ±1-rep noise generated a performance flag in 199/199 stationary-strength control weeks. It becomes a pivot trigger when paired with poor sleep.
  • Coarse equipment blocked 41.1% of recommendations in that scenario.
  • Recurring poor sleep produced a median of 25 pivot weeks, without automatically redesigning the routine.
  • Bodyweight difficulty and effort reporting still need user judgment.
